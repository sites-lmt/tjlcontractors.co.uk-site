Recruiter referrals

If you place contractors, you need a payroll partner who will not let your candidates down. Late payment reflects on you, not on the umbrella.

Why recruiters work with us

We already work with over 1,000 UK agencies, so we understand your side of the process: timesheet deadlines, purchase order references, client invoicing schedules and the fact that a contractor not being paid on time becomes your problem to manage.

Our job is to make sure that never happens. Contractors are paid on time, questions are answered by a named point of contact, and you are not fielding complaints about a provider you recommended.

What that means in practice

  • A single point of contact who knows your contractors and your agency's processes.
  • Reliable payment runs — contractors paid on schedule, with same-day options available.
  • Compliant payroll — everyone paid through PAYE, with no schemes that put your candidates at risk later.
  • Fast onboarding — contractors ready to start without delay at the beginning of an assignment.
  • Invoicing that matches your process — correct references and formats, so nothing sits in a query queue.

What we will not do

We will not approach your contractors behind your back or try to move them onto arrangements you have not agreed. If we are introduced to a contractor through your agency, that relationship stays yours.
