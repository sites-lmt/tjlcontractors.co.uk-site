Umbrella or limited company?

Both are legitimate. They suit different situations, and the difference in take-home is often smaller than people expect.

 UmbrellaLimited company
Admin burdenHandled for youYour responsibility (or your accountant's)
Employment statusEmployee of the umbrellaDirector and employee of your own company
IR35 inside contractsStraightforward and compliantWorks, but the client must be comfortable with it
Short assignmentsWell suitedGenerally not worth the setup cost
Take-homePredictable, easy to calculateCan be higher at larger contract values
