Who needs to file

You will usually need to file a self-assessment return if any of the following apply to you:

  • You are self-employed as a sole trader and earned more than £1,000 in the tax year
  • You are a partner in a business partnership
  • You receive untaxed income — rental income, dividends, savings interest or foreign income
  • You have income from tips or commission that has not been taxed
  • You need to pay the High Income Child Benefit Charge
  • You have sold assets that need to be reported for Capital Gains Tax

If you are paid entirely through PAYE under an umbrella company and have no other income, you normally do not need to file. If you are unsure, ask us — the consultation costs nothing.

What we need from you

Your Unique Taxpayer Reference, your National Insurance number, details of all income for the tax year, and records of anything you intend to claim as an expense. If you are missing something, tell us — it is better to sort it before the deadline than after.

Deadlines to know

Online returns and any tax owed for the previous tax year are due by 31 January. Registering for self-assessment for the first time can take several weeks, so do not leave it until January to begin.
