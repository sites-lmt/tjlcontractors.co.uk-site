Umbrella payroll

An umbrella company employs you directly and handles the invoicing, payroll, tax and National Insurance. Your agency or end client pays the umbrella, the umbrella pays you through PAYE, and you remain an employee with the associated rights.

No. With an umbrella there is no company to set up, no accounts to file and no Companies House returns. That is the point of it.

Yes. You tell your agency where to send payment, we handle the onboarding, and there is no gap in your pay. Nothing in a typical agency contract prevents it.

Once your approved timesheet is received and funds have cleared from your agency, payroll runs. Same-day payment options are available — ask us when you sign up.

Holiday pay accrues at 12.07% of your earnings and is paid to you when you take leave. It shows on your payslip so you can see what has built up.

No. Income tax and National Insurance are deducted once, through PAYE, exactly as they would be in a permanent job. Employer's National Insurance is a separate cost funded from the assignment value, not a second deduction from you.
