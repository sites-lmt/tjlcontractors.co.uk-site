Umbrella payroll, handled end to end

We become your employer. Invoicing, credit control, payroll and compliance all sit with us — you submit a timesheet and get paid through PAYE.

What an umbrella company does

An umbrella company employs you directly. Your agency or end client pays us for the work you have done, and we pay you as an employee — deducting income tax and National Insurance through PAYE and reporting it to HMRC in real time.

That means you keep the employment rights you would expect: holiday pay, statutory sick pay and a proper payslip. You are not running a company, filing accounts or chasing invoices. You are employed, and the admin is somebody else's job.

It suits contractors who want the admin gone, who are on shorter assignments, or who are inside IR35 and need a compliant route to be paid. If you are not sure whether umbrella or a limited company suits you better, ask us — we will give you a straight comparison rather than a sales pitch.

What is included

  • Invoicing, payment allocation and credit control — we raise invoices on your agency, allocate incoming funds and chase anything overdue.
  • Payroll and salary operations — your pay is processed through PAYE, on time, with a full payslip.
  • Same-day payments — available once your approved timesheet is in and agency funds have cleared.
  • Comprehensive insurance plans — professional indemnity and employer's liability cover as standard.

How you get paid

You submit your approved timesheet — weekly or monthly, whichever your agency works to. We invoice, and we chase if the agency is slow. Once funds clear, payroll runs and your money is on its way, with tax and National Insurance already accounted for.

Holiday pay accrues at the statutory rate of 12.07% of your earnings and is paid to you when you take time off.

Switching from another umbrella

Nothing stops you changing umbrella company part-way through an assignment. You tell your agency where to send payment, we handle the onboarding paperwork, and there is no gap in your pay. We will compare your current provider's fees and take-home against ours before you decide.
