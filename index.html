How it works

From timesheet to bank account

Three steps, repeated every week or month. You will always know where your money is.

1

Send us your timesheet

Submit your approved hours online or by email. We check everything is in order and query anything that looks wrong before it becomes a problem.

2

We invoice and chase

We raise the invoice on your agency, allocate the payment and chase anything overdue. Credit control is our job, not yours.

3

You get paid

Once funds clear, we run payroll and pay you through PAYE with a proper payslip. Same-day payment options are available.
