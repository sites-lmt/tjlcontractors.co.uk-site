Before you call

Have these to hand

You do not need any of it to ask a question — but if you want an exact take-home figure, these help us give you one straight away.

  • Your day rate or annual contract value
  • How many days a week you expect to work
  • Whether you are paid weekly or monthly
  • The agency or end client you are contracting through
  • Whether IR35 has been determined for your engagement
  • Your current provider, if you are thinking of switching
